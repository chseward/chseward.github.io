Summary

Staff Genomics Scientist in long-read genetics and epigenetics at the Pacific Northwest Research Institute, in an independent position since 2025. Building on my chromatin and methylation epigenomics with Lisa Stubbs, work with Claudia Carvalho’s group since July 2025 has given me deep experience in clinical human genomics: structural variants, long reads, DNA methylation and rare-disease family studies across more than 2,000 genomes, including the Carvalho lab’s full cohort and GREGoR Consortium data. My work reads structural variants, DNA methylation, X-inactivation and episignatures from PacBio and Oxford Nanopore genomes, with optical mapping and short reads, and applies them to rare X-linked disease, neurodevelopment, and stress and metabolism in pregnancy. I combine bench experience across microbiology, cell biology, neuroscience, imaging, genetics and genomics with computational biology and bioinformatics, and with BSL-2, animal and human clinical research, so my analyses fit the biology and the experimental design; 18 papers and preprints across these fields show it. I bring more than 15 years of combined wet-lab and computational research, and a record of helping move a lab, founding a core and leading collaborative, interdisciplinary projects.

  • Independent position since January 2025; since July 2025 works with Claudia Carvalho’s group, supported by her grants, on XCITE and long-read episignatures (two first-author manuscripts in preparation).
  • Created XCITE, which reads X-inactivation skew from standard PacBio or ONT genomes, agrees with the clinical HUMARA assay (r = 0.97, n = 12) and places structural variants, indels and SNVs on the active or inactive X when phasing allows; in a cohort of more than 80 women, 12 of 16 carrier mothers carried the duplication on the inactive X (ASHG 2026). Preprint and code release in preparation.
